Press Copy signature, then paste it into your email program's signature box. Steps for each program are below.

Gmail (including school Google accounts)

  1. In Gmail on a computer, click the gear icon, then See all settings.
  2. On the General tab, scroll to Signature and click Create new.
  3. Click in the box and paste (Ctrl+V, or Cmd+V on a Mac).
  4. Under Signature defaults, pick it for new emails and replies, then click Save Changes at the bottom.

Outlook

  1. Outlook on the web or new Outlook: gear icon, Account, Signatures. Classic Outlook: File, Options, Mail, Signatures.
  2. Create a new signature, paste into the box, choose it as the default, and save.

iPhone Mail

  1. Open this page on the iPhone, press and hold on the signature, and choose Select All, then Copy.
  2. Go to Settings, Apps, Mail, Signature, and paste.
