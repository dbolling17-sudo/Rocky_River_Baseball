Welcome to the official website of Rocky River High School Baseball.

This site is the place for players, families, alumni and the Rocky River community to find what is happening in the program. Throughout the offseason we will post a monthly calendar of workouts and team activities, followed by the full spring schedule and results once the season begins.

A few things you can do here:

- **Check the [calendar](/calendar/)** for workouts, meetings and team events. You can add it to your phone so updates show up automatically.
- **Meet the [coaching staff](/coaches/)** and learn about our [program](/program/).
- **Follow along in the [news](/news/)** for announcements and game recaps.

Thank you to the River Diamond Club and to every family who supports Rocky River baseball. Let's get to work.

*Coach Devon Bolling*
